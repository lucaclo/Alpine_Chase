# Art Direction Reference

**Source:** 40s TikTok clip by @inkaistudio (`export_1790588080566.MOV`). Key frames saved in `context/reference/ref-01.jpg` … `ref-16.jpg`.
**Priority:** Art style, aesthetic and feel come first in iteration 1. Any visual decision should be checked against these frames.

## Overall look
- **Painterly stylised 3D.** Looks like a hand-painted anime key frame on top of 3D forms (Arcane / Spider-Verse / Ghibli-meets-Steep). It is not photoreal and not flat cel-shaded.
- Visible **brush-stroke texture** on cloth, hair and snow shadows (ref-08, ref-09). The surfaces look like broad painted planes, not fine detail.
- Clean silhouettes, with no heavy black outlines. Edges read through **value contrast** rather than lines.

## Palette
- **Sky:** deep saturated cerulean/cobalt at the top, fading to a pale cyan haze at the horizon.
- **Snow:** warm off-white in the light, **cool periwinkle/blue-violet in the shadows**. Shadows are coloured, never grey.
- **Rock:** dark desaturated charcoal/slate, painted in chunky faceted planes.
- **Character:** navy-blue oversized hoodie with a white splatter graphic, cream knit cuffs, baggy khaki/beige pants, cream bindings, **salmon/terracotta board**, black gloves, white goggles with an iridescent pink/purple lens, spiky platinum-blond windswept hair.
- **Overall:** a limited palette of blue, white and warm accents (skin, board, hair) that pop against the cool environment.

## Lighting
- Bright, clear, high-altitude sun. Strong key light with hard, crisp cast shadows on the snow (ref-05: the rider's shadow is sharp and blue).
- Sun flare/bloom visible (ref-10).
- Warm rim light on the character, with cool fill from the sky.
- Atmospheric perspective: distant peaks fade into blue haze, with a **sea of clouds** below the summit (ref-01, ref-02).

## Terrain & environment
- Big alpine peaks, sharp ridgelines and exposed rock bands.
- Smooth, sculpted snow surfaces with soft wind-lip cornices (ref-14).
- Sparse features: rock outcrops, natural kickers, a large **man-made kicker/jump** (ref-11) and a forested valley far below (ref-11).
- A helicopter drop-in opens the clip (ref-01 to ref-04), which suggests a heli-drop spawn.

## Camera & motion
- Dynamic, cinematic third-person camera: low angles close to the board (ref-06, ref-07), over-the-shoulder shots and chase cam from behind/above (ref-05).
- Strong **speed feel**: snow spray/powder particles off the board edge, wind-blown hair and clothing, slight motion blur and speed lines in the snow (ref-10, ref-15).
- Carving with heavy body lean. The board throws a painted spray plume.

## Effects to replicate
- Powder spray/kick-up particles (painterly clumps, not tiny dots).
- Board trail/track lines in the snow.
- Sun bloom/lens flare.
- Distance haze and a cloud layer below.
- Wind in the hair/clothes (secondary motion).

## Realistic target for real-time
The clip is a pre-rendered/AI-assisted cinematic, so it can't be matched exactly in real time. The aim is to get as close as possible with:
- Custom **toon/stylised shading** with coloured (blue) shadow ramps.
- A **painterly post-process** (e.g. Kuwahara/oil-paint filter, subtle paper/brush texture).
- Stylised, low-to-mid-poly geometry with hand-painted textures.
- Bloom, fog/height haze, and particle snow spray.
